Live demo · SoundLungs

Connected data. Verified before AI.

A Raspberry Pi sensor and NVIDIA Jetson each use an Ankhor hardware root. The Jetson analyzes a recording only after every required identity, session, encryption, and provenance check passes.

One visible policy

If the hardware root is absent, the enrolled identity is different, the session proof fails, or the signed bytes do not match, AI stays locked.

Failure scenario

What if the enrolled root of trust is missing?

An encrypted channel can still carry untrusted input. Without pinned hardware identity and session-bound provenance, a substituted endpoint could replay or modify a recording and present false context.

Secure transport is not trusted input.TLS protects a connection; it does not by itself prove the physical sensor, exact recording, or session provenance.
A valid different key is still wrong.The demo compares immutable identity and enrolled public material, so swapping working keys triggers the compromised state.
C2PA is evidence, not truth.It makes configured provenance tamper-evident under policy; it does not determine whether audio content is factually true.

Three different hardware-key roles+

The sensor key proves the Raspberry Pi identity, the processor key proves the Jetson identity, and a third browser-connected key authorizes operator actions. These roles are not interchangeable.

What is inside hardware?+

PUF-derived roots, pinned device identity, and selected protected signing and post-quantum key operations. The exact boundary depends on the product configuration.

What remains at system level?+

TLS, bulk AES streaming, C2PA packaging and policy verification, the browser interface, orchestration, and the AI admission policy.

What does C2PA prove?+

It provides verifiable, tamper-evident provenance under a chosen trust policy. It does not prove that the recording is factually or clinically true.

SoundLungs is an engineering exhibition demonstrator, not a medical device. It makes no diagnostic or clinical-performance claim.
